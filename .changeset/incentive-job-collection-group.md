New `incentive` group — a job-collection resource beyond basedata's own endpoints (doc 20 §5.7; job-collections foundation design), landing after `concern`. An array of per-job instances, each with `fromTemplateUuid` and `amount`: per the runtime-reference-resolution decision, `fromTemplateUuid` is captured as PLAIN free text (the spoken rebate/program name), never a lookup/enum/UUID leaf — resolution against the program's template list is deferred to review/write time. The endpoint's third field, `recommendations`, is excluded from extraction entirely (the recommendations tranche is dropped: no write endpoint exists for it). Instance identity is wired through the existing roster mechanism: the inventory pass gains an `incentive` group with a granularity rule distinguishing one incentive from another by the rebate/program name mentioned and/or its amount, and the extraction instructions gain rule 15 for the free-text capture. The schema totals 213 leaves across nine groups; `descriptions.generated.ts` was regenerated (vendor descriptions now compose onto both new leaves) and the frozen extraction fixture was re-captured for this deliberate field-set change.
