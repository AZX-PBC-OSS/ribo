Job-collections tranche 6 (doc 20 §5.5): the `wall` group grows to its endpoint's full verdicted capture surface — every base-side and improved-side `capture`/`capture?` field, skipping only the self-described computed ones (`wallAssemblyRValue` and its improved twin, "not editable since it is calculated from the other fields", on a vendor description copy-pasted from the basement wall field) — and a new `door` group lands as a per-instance array on the same pattern, carrying every field of `POST /jobs/{jobId}/door` (doc 20 excludes none of them). Quirks matched to the letter: `wallExteriorWallConstruction` is an UNPUBLISHED enum (`enum: []` — a named dropdown with no member list), so a dictated construction is captured as free text into review; `wallCavityInsulationTypeImproved` reuses the `WallCavityInsulationType` export (the spec publishes the identical list, leading literal `null` included, which the leaf's `.nullable()` already models); the two continuous-insulation-type fields share one new `WallContinuousInsulationType` export (doc 20 §5.0's RIGID-INSULATION-TYPES); and `doorType` is the rich enum encoding material, insulation, half-lite glazing and storm door in ONE string — the vendor's own inconsistent storm casing ("Steel, hollow with storm" vs "Steel, insulated with Storm") verbatim — with new rule 17 teaching the model to combine the separately-spoken facts into the single matching member. Door instance identity rides the existing roster mechanism (foundation design decision 2) with the door type/construction as the handle, but door is the WEAKEST FIT in the family: no name, no location AND no count field — `doorArea` is a single aggregate the vendor describes as overridable "with the actual door sizes for the home" (plural) — so two same-type doors have no wire-visible separation and the resource may need real design attention later. Door's routing rule is encoded on `doorArea` and in rule 17: a door glazed at or above half its rough opening is modelled as a WINDOW at rough-opening area — that fact belongs to the window group, never to `doorArea`. The inventory pass gains a `door` group with a granularity rule. `descriptions.generated.ts` was regenerated via `pnpm snugg:refresh` (the vendor describes every new leaf) and the frozen extraction fixture was deliberately re-captured for this field-set change (the diff confined to the wall group's properties and required list plus the new door group); the wall group now exceeds the 8-enveloped-leaf per-call cap and partitions across calls. The ground-truth corpus is untouched by design — unannotated leaves score as unmentioned.
