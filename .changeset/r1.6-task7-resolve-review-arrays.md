R1.6 Task 7 — `resolveReview` reassembles arrays, and produces the three patch states

`resolveReview` now parses review paths with `parseFieldPath(...)` and rebuilds
collection groups as arrays. A named `buildInstancePositions` step remaps stable
instance keys (`k1`, `k2`, ...) to positional indices before the patch is built,
so removing an instance cannot reattach another instance's decisions.

The three patch states are now distinguishable:

- a group whose every instance leaf was rejected is absent from the patch;
- a group that was present-but-empty in the extraction becomes `[]`;
- an accepted instance becomes `[{...}, ...]` in first-mention order.
